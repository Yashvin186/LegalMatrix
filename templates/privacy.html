STATUTORY PRIVACY GOVERNANCE

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 2026 | Governing Legislation: Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 & Information Technology Act, 2000

1. Purpose and Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how LegalMetriX collects, processes, and protects visual data and metadata submitted to our compliance inspection system. LegalMetriX is engineered for regulatory compliance screening of packaged commodities under the Legal Metrology Act, 2009 and the Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Rules, 2011.

2. Nature of Data Processed

LegalMetriX processes the following technical and packaging artifacts:

  • Packaging Images: High-resolution photographs of Principal Display Panels (PDP), information panels, and barcode areas uploaded by users or captured via live device camera.
  • Optical OCR Extractions: Text strings recognized from label surfaces, including Manufacturer Name, Address, Maximum Retail Price (MRP), Unit Sale Price (USP), Net Quantity, Manufacturing/Expiry Dates, and Consumer Care details.
  • Technical Diagnostic Logs: Image resolution, sharpness index, blur metrics, and processing timestamps necessary to determine visual inspection legibility.

LegalMetriX does not collect personally identifiable individual consumer records (PII) during automated packaging verification workflows.

3. Image Processing & Retention Protocol

Uploaded label images are ingested solely for statutory compliance analysis, cross-panel coherence verification, and preliminary report compilation. Images uploaded into temporary session storage are scrubbed automatically following processing completion or session expiry. We do not sell, rent, or commercialize uploaded imagery or proprietary packaging formulations.

4. Public Web Verification Sources

During Authenticity Risk evaluation, LegalMetriX queries public, publicly indexable brand catalogs and e-commerce repositories solely to cross-reference declared net weight, MRP, and manufacturer identity. No user credentials or session identifiers are transmitted to third-party search endpoints.

5. Data Security Standards

LegalMetriX enforces strict access control policies, TLS encryption in transit for all client-server communications, memory boundary isolation during image parsing, and secure execution of computer vision pipelines to safeguard against data interception and cross-tenant leakage.

6. Regulatory Authority Inquiries

Where inspection reports are exported as statutory inspection dossiers by authorized Legal Metrology inspectors or enforcement agencies, such records remain subject to official departmental recordkeeping mandates and the Indian Evidence Act / Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam standards.

7. Amendments & Contact

This policy is maintained to align continuously with notifications issued by the Department of Consumer Affairs and Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY). For privacy inquiries or compliance auditing questions, please submit inquiries via official organizational communication channels.
